# NitroJam Clip Bot

NitroJam Clip Bot is a private tool used only by the streamer NitroJam (kick.com/nitrojam). When a viewer types !clip in the NitroJam Kick chat, the tool saves the last 30 seconds of the stream on the streamer's own computer, edits it into a vertical short video, and uploads it to the NitroJam YouTube channel and to the streamer's own Google Drive folder.
